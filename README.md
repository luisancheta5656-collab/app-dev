# My Favorite Series: Gintama

## About
**Gintama** is a Japanese anime and manga series created by *Hideaki Sorachi*.
It's set in an alternate Edo-period Japan that has been taken over by aliens called the **Amanto**,
and samurai are no longer allowed to carry swords.

> A comedy that can switch to serious action and drama at any moment.

---

## Main Characters
| Character | Role |
|-----------|------|
| Gintoki Sakata | Lazy ex-samurai and leader of the Yorozuya |
| Shinpachi Shimura | Glasses-wearing assistant of Gintoki |
| Kagura | Super-strong girl from the Yato clan |
| Sadaharu | Giant dog of the Yorozuya |

## Why I Like It
1. The comedy and parodies are hilarious
2. The serious arcs are full of emotion and great fights
3. The characters feel like a real family
